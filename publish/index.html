Who I Am

My name is Prajna. I'm a software developer with a deep interest in how intelligent systems can be built to solve real-world problems. I build across the full stack — from clean, responsive frontends to backend APIs and machine learning pipelines.

My recent work focuses on the intersection of geospatial data and AI, including CNN-based satellite image analysis for agricultural monitoring and thermal super-resolution for environmental sensing. I care a lot about the engineering underneath the interface: latency, maintainability, and the architecture that holds it together.

Outside of projects, I'm continuously learning — whether that's a new paper on vision transformers, a systems design concept, or a new framework worth understanding.
